# Mechanic Scams & How to Avoid Them — India

## Common Scams at Indian Service Centers

### 1. Unnecessary Parts Replacement

**Scam:** Mechanic says your air filter, chain, battery, or clutch plates need replacement when they don't.

**How to check:**
- Air filter: Can visually inspect — grey is ok, black and blocked = replace. Ask to see old part.
- Chain: Use the 1-inch lift test — if chain lifts more than 1cm from rear sprocket, it's worn.
- Battery: Test voltage with multimeter yourself (₹200 multimeters available).

**Red flag:** Mechanic cannot show you the part being replaced or keeps the old part.

### 2. Engine Oil Scam

**Scam:** Using cheap/substandard oil and charging for premium oil. Topping up instead of full drain.

**Protection:**
- Buy your own oil from automotive parts store (e.g., Motul, Castrol, Shell Advance)
- Bring it to the workshop and watch the pour
- Check oil grade: BS6 bikes typically need 10W-30 or 10W-40 MA2 rated

**Typical oil change cost truth:**
- 1L synthetic: ₹350–₹600
- Filter: ₹80–₹150
- Labour: ₹100–₹200
- Total fair price: ₹550–₹950

Authorized centers often charge ₹1,200–₹2,000 for the same — this is margin, not scam, but worth knowing.

### 3. Carburetor "Overhauling" Scam (older bikes)

**Scam:** Claiming carb overhaul needed for minor issues. Charging ₹800–₹1,500 when only cleaning was done.

**Reality:** Carb cleaning takes 30–45 minutes. A true overhaul involves replacing jets, needle, float valve. Parts cost ₹200–₹400. If charged ₹1,500+, ask for itemized bill.

### 4. BS6 ECU "Reflash" Scam

**Scam:** Unauthorized shops claim ECU flash will improve mileage by 20–30%. Often a fake process that charges ₹2,000–₹5,000.

**Reality:** Only authorized dealers have proper OEM flash tools. Unauthorized flashes can damage ECU or void warranty. Mileage improvement claims are mostly false.

### 5. Bent Frame After Accident

**Scam:** After a minor tip-over, claiming frame is bent and needs replacement (₹15,000–₹40,000+).

**Reality:** Most frames survive minor drops. Get independent inspection from a trusted mechanic or ask for written assessment before agreeing.

### 6. "Genuine Parts" But Aftermarket

Selling spurious parts (fake OEM) at genuine OEM prices.

**Protection:**
- Buy parts from authorized dealer spare parts counter yourself
- Check hologram stickers on parts like HERO Genuine Parts, Bajaj OEM, Honda genuine
- Check part number matches manufacturer catalog

---

## Questions to Ask Your Mechanic

1. "Can you show me the old part before replacing it?"
2. "Can I see the job card / estimate before starting any work?"
3. "Is this covered under warranty?" (BS6 bikes: 5-year warranty typical)
4. "What exactly is included in this 'free service'?" (First 3 services are free at authorized centers)

---

## Finding Trustworthy Mechanics

- **Authorized service centers:** More expensive but accountable, warranty preserved
- **Trusted independent:** Ask in local bike owner groups (Facebook, WhatsApp groups for specific bike models)
- **Get written estimate** before any repair above ₹1,000
- **Compare quotes** — genuine parts prices are fixed, labour should be comparable within ±30%
