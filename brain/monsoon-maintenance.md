# Monsoon Maintenance & Common Issues — India

## Pre-Monsoon Checklist

Before the rains hit (April-May), check:

1. **Tyres** — Replace if below 1.6mm tread depth. Wet roads demand grip.
   - Budget tyres: ₹1,200–₹2,000
   - Premium (MRF Mogrip, CEAT Zoom): ₹2,000–₹4,500

2. **Brake pads/shoes** — Replace if near minimum. Wet braking distance increases 40–60%

3. **Chain lubrication** — Use wet-rated chain lube (not WD-40 which washes off)
   - Re-lubricate every 300–500km in monsoon (vs 700km dry season)
   - Cost: ₹150–₹400 per can

4. **Air filter** — Inspect and clean. Wet air filter causes rich running and power loss.

5. **Electrical connectors** — Apply dielectric grease to all connectors, especially headlight, tail lamp

6. **Battery terminals** — Clean and apply terminal grease/petroleum jelly

## Monsoon-Specific Problems

### Waterlogged Road Engine Hydro-Lock

Never attempt to start a bike that has been submerged or ridden through very deep water.

If water entered through air intake:
- Remove spark plug immediately
- Crank engine without plug to expel water
- Change engine oil (water contaminated = milky oil)
- Let dry before restarting

Signs of hydro-lock: Engine suddenly stops, will not turn over at all, hard kick

Cost if engine damaged from hydro-lock: ₹5,000–₹20,000+ (connecting rod bend, piston/liner damage)

### Rust and Corrosion

Indian monsoon causes rapid corrosion on:
- Disc brake rotors (surface rust after overnight — clears after a few stops, normal)
- Chain (if not lubricated)
- Exposed bolt heads
- Exhaust pipes/silencer

Prevention:
- Park under cover
- Apply ACF-50 or Corrosion-X to electricals
- Chain: use O-ring chain + proper wet lube

### Slipping Clutch in Heavy Rain

Wet clutch plates (normal multi-plate design) can slip temporarily in extreme water ingress through engine breather — usually self-resolves.

Persistent clutch slip:
- Worn clutch plates (normal wear: replace at 40,000–60,000 km)
- Wrong engine oil viscosity
- Clutch cable maladjusted (too tight)

Cost:
- Clutch plate set: ₹800–₹2,500
- Cable replacement: ₹100–₹300

## Post-Monsoon Checks (October)

1. **Full chain inspection** — clean, measure chain slack, replace chain+sprocket if stretched
   - Chain+sprocket kit: ₹800–₹2,500

2. **Fork seals** — check for leaks exacerbated by wet riding

3. **Brake fluid** — replace if it has absorbed moisture (test strips available ₹100–₹200)

4. **Air filter replacement** — a season of dusty+wet riding clogs filters thoroughly

5. **Full service including spark plug** — prepare for dry season performance
