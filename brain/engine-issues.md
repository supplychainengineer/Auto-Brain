# Engine Issues — Indian Two-Wheelers

## Hard Starting (Kick/Self-Start Failure)

### BS6 FI (Fuel Injected) Bikes
Common causes:
- Weak or discharged battery (most common) — BS6 bikes require stronger batteries for fuel pump and ECU
- Faulty fuel pump — listen for priming buzz when ignition is ON
- Clogged fuel injector — especially after long storage or bad petrol
- Throttle position sensor (TPS) error
- Idle air control valve (IACV) clogged with carbon

Cost in INR:
- Battery replacement: ₹800–₹2,500
- Fuel pump: ₹2,500–₹6,000
- Injector cleaning (ultrasonic): ₹500–₹1,200
- TPS sensor: ₹1,500–₹4,000

### Carburetor Bikes (older models, BS4 and below)
- Clogged main jet or pilot jet
- Choke stuck closed
- Float bowl overflow due to worn needle valve
- Stale petrol after monsoon non-use

Cost in INR:
- Carb cleaning: ₹200–₹500
- Jet replacement: ₹50–₹200
- Float needle valve: ₹100–₹300

## Engine Overheating

Especially common during summer and in slow city traffic.

Causes:
- Low engine oil or degraded oil (most common)
- Clogged radiator fins (air-cooled) or coolant issues (liquid-cooled)
- Wrong fuel octane — use minimum 91 RON as recommended
- Lean fuel mixture (especially after monsoon air filter clog)
- Broken or missing heat shield
- Riding on reserve fuel frequently

Warning signs:
- Strong fuel smell while riding
- Power loss with heat soak
- Ticking noise getting louder

Cost:
- Oil change (engine oil + filter): ₹300–₹800
- Air filter replacement: ₹150–₹500
- Coolant flush (liquid-cooled): ₹400–₹800

## Engine Knocking / Pinging

Metallic knocking sound especially under load (climbing slopes, overtaking).

Causes:
- Low octane petrol — avoid mixing from different pumps
- Carbon buildup causing hot spots (common after 15,000+ km)
- Wrong spark plug heat range
- Ignition timing off (older carb bikes)
- Overheating

Urgency: HIGH — prolonged knocking causes piston/bore damage.

Cost:
- Spark plug replacement: ₹100–₹800 depending on type (standard vs iridium)
- Decarbonization (valve, piston): ₹1,000–₹2,500
- Top overhaul if worn piston rings: ₹3,000–₹7,000

---

## Black Smoke from Exhaust

Indicates rich fuel mixture.

Causes:
- Clogged air filter (most common in Indian dusty roads)
- BS6 lambda/O2 sensor fault
- Leaking fuel injector
- Choke stuck (carb bikes)

Cost:
- Air filter cleaning/replacement: ₹100–₹500
- O2 sensor: ₹800–₹2,500
- Injector service: ₹500–₹1,200

## White/Blue Smoke

White smoke: coolant entering combustion = head gasket issue (serious)
Blue smoke: oil burning = worn piston rings or valve seals

Cost:
- Head gasket: ₹500–₹1,500 (part) + ₹1,500–₹3,000 labour
- Piston ring set + boring: ₹2,000–₹6,000
