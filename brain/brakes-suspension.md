# Brakes and Suspension — Indian Two-Wheelers

## Brake Issues

### Squealing / Screeching Brakes

Very common in monsoon and dusty roads.

Causes (ranked by frequency):
1. Glazed brake pads — surface hardened from heat cycles, polish with sandpaper
2. Dust/grit embedded in pads — clean with compressed air
3. Worn brake pads below minimum thickness (2mm)
4. Cheap aftermarket pads — use OEM or reputed brands (TVS, Brembo, Endurance)
5. Warped disc rotor (drum brake shoe glazing equivalent for older bikes)

Cost:
- Brake pad set (disc): ₹200–₹800
- Brake shoe set (drum): ₹100–₹400
- Disc rotor replacement: ₹800–₹2,500
- Brake disc skimming (trueing): ₹200–₹400

### Spongy / Soft Brake Lever (Disc Brakes)

Causes:
- Air in brake lines — needs bleeding
- Low brake fluid — check reservoir
- Brake fluid moisture absorption (hygroscopic) — replace every 2 years
- Worn master cylinder seals
- Leaking caliper seal

Urgency: HIGH — do not ride until fixed.

Cost:
- Brake bleeding + fluid top-up: ₹200–₹400
- Master cylinder rebuild kit: ₹300–₹600
- Caliper seal kit: ₹200–₹500
- Full caliper replacement: ₹1,500–₹4,000

### ABS Warning Light ON (Modern BS6 bikes)

Causes:
- Wheel speed sensor dirty or faulty (especially rear, exposed to mud)
- Damaged sensor wire
- ABS module fault (rare)

Cost:
- Sensor cleaning: ₹0 (DIY with cloth)
- Wheel speed sensor replacement: ₹800–₹2,500

## Suspension Issues

### Front Fork Oil Leak

Very common after 20,000 km, especially on rough Indian roads.

Symptoms: Oil stains on fork legs, seals visible, soft/bottoming front end

Causes:
- Worn fork seals (expected wear)
- Bent fork tube from pothole impact
- Dust/grit scoring the tube

Cost:
- Fork seal replacement (per fork): ₹300–₹600 parts + ₹500–₹800 labour
- Fork oil change: ₹200–₹500 (with seal replacement)
- Fork tube replacement if bent: ₹1,500–₹4,000

### Rear Shock Absorber — Bouncy/Soft

Common at 30,000–40,000 km or after heavy pillion+luggage use.

Causes:
- Worn shock absorber (oil seal degraded, no damping)
- Spring sag from overloading
- Preload incorrectly set for Indian potholes

Cost:
- Rear shock pair (aftermarket — Gabriel, Endurance, YSS): ₹1,500–₹4,000
- OEM replacement: ₹2,500–₹7,000

Recommended for Indian roads: Set preload 1–2 notches higher than default.

### Handlebar Wobble / Shimmy

Urgency: VERY HIGH — safety hazard

Causes:
- Wheel bearing worn out (check by lifting front wheel and spinning — should be smooth)
- Loose steering head bearings
- Tyre balance off
- Bent rim from pothole
- Tyre pressure imbalance

Cost:
- Wheel balancing: ₹50–₹100 per wheel
- Tyre replacement (if cupped/worn): ₹1,500–₹4,000
- Wheel bearing set: ₹200–₹600
- Steering head bearing: ₹200–₹800
